My Recipe Book

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

1. About this policy

This policy explains how My Recipe Book handles information when you use the My Recipe Book website or app. My Recipe Book is a personal recipe-organizing tool.

2. Recipe information and device storage

Recipes, ingredients, directions, categories, notes, favourites, and related app information that you enter are stored in your browser's local storage on the device you use. This information is intended to remain on your device unless you choose to export, share, restore, or sync a backup. Clearing the browser's site data, uninstalling the app, or losing access to the device may remove locally stored information. Keep a separate backup of important recipes.

3. Optional Google Drive backup and sync

If you choose to connect Google Drive, My Recipe Book uses Google sign-in and the Google Drive API to create, update, and restore the app's recipe backup in your Google Drive. The app requests the Google Drive drive.file permission, which is limited to files created by or opened with the app. Your backup is stored in the Google account you select. Google handles the sign-in process and the storage of files in Google Drive under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

Google Drive backup and sync are optional. You can use the app without connecting Google Drive. If you enable automatic sync, the app may transfer backup updates to Google Drive while the app is running and an authorized connection is available.

4. Import tools and third-party services

The app loads Google Identity Services for Google sign-in and uses the PDF.js and Tesseract.js libraries, delivered through public content-delivery networks, for document and image text-import features. These libraries run in your browser to support the import tools. Your use of Google services is also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy information.

5. How information is used

Information entered into the app is used to provide recipe organization, search, editing, import, export, backup, restore, and sync features. The app is not intended to sell your recipe information or use it for advertising.

6. Sharing and backups

You control whether to share or export a recipe or backup using your device's sharing and file-saving features. If you share a file or connect a Google account, the information may be handled by the service or people you choose to share it with. Review the destination and recipients before sharing.

7. Security and your choices

Protect access to your device and Google account. You can choose not to connect Google Drive, turn off automatic sync in the app, and delete local app data using your device or browser settings. Deleting local data does not automatically delete a backup already stored in Google Drive; manage that file separately in Google Drive.

8. Children's privacy

My Recipe Book is a general-purpose recipe tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
