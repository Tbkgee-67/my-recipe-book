🌡️ Cooking Temperatures

🌡️ Cooking Temperatures

🛡️ About these temperatures

Green labels mark USDA safe minimum internal temperatures. Measure with a food thermometer in the thickest part, away from bone and fat.

Grey labels mark preference values, such as steak doneness and barbecue pull temperatures. They are not food-safety recommendations.

Carryover cooking: food may continue rising about 5–10°F (3–6°C) after coming off the heat. Large roasts may need to be removed a little below the target.

Safe minimums summarized from USDA FSIS / FoodSafety.gov guidance. Follow local guidance and product-specific instructions where applicable.

Import recipes

Import a recipe from a text file, PDF, photo, or scanned page. OCR reads text from recipe pictures and scanned PDFs. Imported recipes open for review before saving.

OCR needs an internet connection to load the text-recognition engine and English language data. Results may need correction, especially for handwriting or blurry photos. Gmail: copy the recipe from the email and paste it here.

Backup / Restore

Download a JSON backup and keep it somewhere safe.

Google Drive backup

Connect Google Drive to back up your recipes. Optional automatic sync uploads changes while this app is open and Google Drive is connected. Each device must be connected separately.

When enabled, changes sync after a short pause. On a new app session, connect Google Drive again to resume syncing. Keep your Drive backup as a safety copy.

Choose save location works only in browsers that support a file picker. Share / save backup may let you send the file to Google Drive or another app. Otherwise, download the backup and move it using My Files.

Restore / merge a JSON backup. Existing recipes are kept; matching IDs are updated.

Restoring a backup and deleting all recipes require your 4-digit PIN. For Google Drive, download this backup and upload it to Drive. Automatic sync works only while Google Drive is connected in the current app session.
