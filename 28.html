How to use the videos: Press play on the short clip in each daily card. The clip starts at the relevant movement and is trimmed to a short section where possible. Use the written instructions and movement cue alongside the demonstration. If a clip does not load, use the “Open video” link.

Safety: Use a sturdy chair without wheels and keep both feet supported. Move slowly, stay in a comfortable range and breathe normally. Never force a stretch or twist. Skip any movement that causes pain. Optional standing is not necessary; if attempted, hold a stable chair and have another person nearby if balance is uncertain. Stop for sharp pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or unusual breathlessness. This is general wellness guidance, not individualized rehabilitation.
